Our Story

A taste of home, from Haiti to Camden.

Chevalier is a Black-owned and operated Haitian Caribbean kitchen on Grant Street in Camden. We cook the food we grew up on — griyo, kabrit, legim, lalo and ke bèf — with the bold, home-style flavor of Haiti.

We're take-out friendly, so everything is made to travel: order online and swing by Grant Street, or order on Uber Eats, DoorDash or Grubhub and have a hot plate of griyo, legim or diri djondjon brought to your door.
